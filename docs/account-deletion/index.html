Delete your STRATA account

STRATA is a geology field app for Montana and Wyoming. This page explains how to delete your account and what happens to your data.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your rock catalog, including notes and saved locations
  • Every rock photo you took
  • Your identification history and its GPS locations
  • Your account and the email address used to sign in

Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. Copies may remain in our hosting provider's backups for a limited time before those backups are overwritten. Nothing is kept for any other purpose.

Delete from the app

  1. Open STRATA and tap Catalog.
  2. Tap Account in the top corner.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If you never added an email, you're using a guest account, which is tied to the app on your phone. Delete it from the app as described above. Uninstalling the app does not delete it.
